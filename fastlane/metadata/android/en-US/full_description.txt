OpenTune is an unofficial open source music player for TIDAL, with local playback.

OpenTune has no ads, analytics or tracking.

<ul>
<li>Stream TIDAL in lossless FLAC, up to 24-bit/192 kHz where TIDAL provides it, and see the format of the playing track.</li>
<li>Home, mixes, radio, new releases, artist and album pages with biographies and credits, and music videos.</li>
<li>Your library: saved tracks, albums, artists, playlists and folders. Create and edit playlists.</li>
<li>Lyrics that follow the song, and autoplay when the queue ends.</li>
<li>Download tracks, optionally download saved or played tracks automatically, and listen without a connection.</li>
<li>Add audio files and folders. They stay up to date, show their format and location, and play in the same player as TIDAL.</li>
<li>Twelve themes with a live preview, a sleep timer, volume normalization and a queue that you reorder by dragging.</li>
<li>Controls in the notification, on the lock screen, on Bluetooth devices and in Android Auto, and a home screen widget.</li>
</ul>

You sign in on TIDAL's website in your browser. OpenTune never receives your password.

OpenTune is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TIDAL. Streaming requires a TIDAL subscription. TIDAL can change or block the interfaces OpenTune uses without notice.
